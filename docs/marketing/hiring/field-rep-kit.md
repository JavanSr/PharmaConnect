# APOTEKH Field Rep Kit — Dodoma

The rep's single source of truth. Everything here is approved language — the rep
does not invent claims, prices, or discounts. If a question isn't answered here,
the answer is: *"Nitakuletea jibu leo — nauliza timu"* and escalate to Elihaki
on WhatsApp.

**Hard rules (same as the whole company):**
- Prices are fixed: ADDO 15,000 · BASIC 39,000 · STANDARD 55,000 · PREMIUM 75,000
  Tsh/month. Annual = 10× (2 months free). 6 months = 5.5×. 3 months = 3×.
  **No discounts, ever.** The only lever is the longer billing cycle.
- Never mention: EFDMS/TRA (retail), NHIF claims, CPD points, "open marketplace".
- Never badmouth DukaDawa/Famasia by name. Compare on what we do, not what they don't.
- Patient data: our system stores NO patient names/IDs — session-based safety
  checks only. This is a feature; say it proudly when asked about privacy.
- Payment is inside the app (M-Pesa/Tigo Pesa/Airtel STK push). The rep NEVER
  collects money, never handles a customer's phone during payment PIN entry.

## 2. The 15-minute counter demo (script)

Do the demo on YOUR device with the demo account. Never rush past step 4 — it's
the moment that sells.

1. **Open (1 min):** one-liner + "Nikuonyeshe kwa dakika kumi tu, kwenye simu hii."
2. **Stock intake (3 min):** scan a product with the camera → batch + expiry
   auto-filled → "Angalia — imekwambia batch hii ina-expire lini, na itakukumbusha."
3. **Dispensing (3 min):** sell 2 items → payment method screen (Cash/M-Pesa) →
   receipt. "Hakuna daftari, hakuna kukariri bei."
4. **★ THE MOMENT (3 min):** dispense two interacting drugs → the CONTRAINDICATED
   alert fires → pause, let them read it. Then:
   "Hii ndiyo tofauti. Hakuna famasi Tanzania inayokagua mwingiliano wa dawa
   wakati wa kuuza. Hii inalinda mgonjwa wako — na leseni yako."
5. **Owner Dashboard (2 min):** show today's revenue + low stock on the phone.
   "Hii ndiyo simu ya mmiliki akiwa safarini."
6. **Close (3 min):** "Tukuanzishie jaribio la siku 14 sasa hivi? Dakika tano,
   bure, hakuna kadi." → register them on THEIR phone, on the spot. Never leave
   a 'maybe' without a scheduled return date, written down.

**Offline proof trick:** put your phone in airplane mode mid-demo and keep
dispensing. It's the single most convincing 10 seconds for upcountry owners.

- Split Dodoma into 5 zones (CBD/Machinga, Uzunguni/hospitali, Area C/D,
  Kikuyu/Ipagala, Chang'ombe/Makole — adjust with local knowledge). One zone per
  weekday; revisit cycle every 2 weeks.
- Target: **8–10 visits/day**, of which ≥3 full demos. Mornings = new visits;
  afternoons = follow-ups and trial check-ins.

  and day 12 (decision conversation) — synchronized with the WhatsApp nurture

## 5. Tracking & commission (how the rep gets paid)

- Every visit is a row in the prospect sheet (`docs/marketing/prospect-sheet-template.csv`):
  status moves NOT_CONTACTED → CONTACTED → DEMO_DONE → TRIAL → PAYING → LOST.
- **Verification is automatic:** trials and payments appear in the APOTEKH admin
  dashboard. A trial "counts" when the pharmacy records ≥1 real dispensing.
  A conversion "counts" when the subscription shows ACTIVE (paid via app).
- Commission paid monthly against the dashboard numbers, not the sheet.
- Disputes resolved by the data. No dashboard record = no commission.

## 6. What the rep must never do

- Promise features that don't exist (check the Claims Library; when unsure, ask).
- Give discounts, "free months", or accept cash for subscriptions.
- Handle the customer's phone during PIN entry, or register a pharmacy with the
  rep's own phone number as the owner contact.
- Speak about NHIF, EFDMS/TRA, CPD points, or "coming soon" features to close a sale.
- Trash competitors by name.
- Leave a demo without either a trial started or a written next appointment.
