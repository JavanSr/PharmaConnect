# Trial Nurture Sequence — 7 WhatsApp messages, verbatim

Sent manually per trial, timed from the trial start date in the prospect sheet.
Sender: the executor (helper AI/assistant account) or the rep — ONE of them per
pharmacy, never both. Log every send in the sheet. Personalise only the
[bracketed] parts; do not rewrite the copy. Kiswahili is the default; switch to
English only if the customer writes in English first.

**Day 0 — Karibu (within 1 hour of trial start)**
> Karibu APOTEKH, [jina]! 🎉 Akaunti ya [jina la famasi] iko tayari.
> Anza hapa: weka bidhaa zako 10 zinazouzwa zaidi — inachukua dakika 10 tu.
> Ukikwama popote, nijibu hapa hapa. Nipo.

**Day 2 — Setup check**
> Habari [jina]! Umeweza kuingiza stoku yako? Kama bado, nikupigie nikusaidie —
> dakika 15 tu tunamaliza pamoja. Pia jaribu ku-scan bidhaa moja kwa kamera 📱

**Day 5 — Owner Dashboard spotlight**
> [Jina], kitu kimoja leo: fungua *Owner Dashboard* asubuhi kesho kabla
> hujafika dukani. Utaona mauzo ya jana, dawa zinazoisha, na zinazokaribia
> ku-expire — bila kumuuliza mtu. Wamiliki wengi wanasema hii ndiyo sehemu
> wanayopenda zaidi.

**Day 8 — Safety moment**
> Je, umeshaona onyo la mwingiliano wa dawa (drug interaction alert) likijitokeza
> ukiwa unauza? Hilo ndilo linalofanya APOTEKH iwe tofauti — linamlinda mgonjwa
> wako na leseni yako. Kila mpango unalo, hata wa 15,000. Endelea kuuza kawaida —
> mfumo unakulinda nyuma yako.

**Day 11 — Value recap + how to pay**
> [Jina], siku 3 zimebaki kwenye jaribio lako. Ukiamua kuendelea:
> Fungua app → *Settings → Subscription* → chagua mpango → weka namba yako ya
> M-Pesa/Tigo Pesa → thibitisha kwenye simu yako. Inakamilika chini ya dakika moja.
> Mipango: ADDO 15,000 · BASIC 39,000 · STANDARD 55,000 · PREMIUM 75,000 kwa mwezi.
> Ukilipa mwaka mzima unapata miezi 2 bure; miezi 6 unapunguza 8%.

**Day 13 — Trial ends tomorrow**
> [Jina], jaribio linaisha kesho. Kulipia ni dakika moja ndani ya app — au kama
> una swali lolote kwanza, nipigie sasa hivi tuongee dakika 10. Nisikupoteze! 🙏

**Day 15 — Lapsed (only if they didn't pay)**
> [Jina], jaribio limeisha lakini mlango uko wazi — data yako yote iko salama
> na akaunti inasubiri. Swali moja tu (linanisaidia sana): nini kilikuzuia
> kuendelea? Bei, muda, au kitu kingine? Jibu moja tu la neno linatosha.

## Rules
- One question or one action per message. Never send two messages in a day.
- Day 15's answer goes into the objection library and the sheet's LOST reason.
- If a pharmacy pays before day 11, stop the sequence and send a single thank-you
  + "nikufundishe [feature inayofuata]?" message.
- Never pressure past day 15. One re-engagement at day 45 max (new case study or
  feature as the reason), then leave them alone.
