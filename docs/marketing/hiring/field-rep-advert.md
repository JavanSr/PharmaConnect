## Kazi yenyewe

APOTEKH ni mfumo wa kisasa wa kuendesha famasi — stoku na tarehe za mwisho (FEFO),
mauzo, ukaguzi wa mwingiliano wa dawa (drug interactions), ripoti za mmiliki, na
compliance — unaofanya kazi hata bila intaneti, kwenye simu ya kawaida.
Mfumo upo hai, famasi zinaweza kujisajili na kulipia zenyewe kwa M-Pesa/Tigo
Pesa/Airtel Money ndani ya app.

Kazi yako: **kuingia mtaani.** Kutembelea famasi na maduka ya dawa (ADDO) Dodoma,
kuonyesha mfumo kwenye simu/tablet pale pale kaunta, kuwasajili kwenye jaribio la
siku 14, na kuwafuatilia mpaka walipie.

**Siyo kazi ya social media.** Content na mitandao vinashughulikiwa na timu.
Wewe ni miguu, macho, na sauti ya APOTEKH mtaani.

# JOB OPENING — Pharmaceutical Field Sales Representative, Dodoma (English summary)

APOTEKH is a live pharmacy operating system (offline-first inventory/FEFO,
dispensing with drug-interaction checking, owner dashboards, compliance) with
self-service onboarding and in-app mobile-money payment. We need a
**pharmacy-trained field rep** (pharmaceutical technician/assistant or pharmacy
graduate; med-rep experience a plus) to walk Dodoma's pharmacy streets: 8–10
counter visits a day, 15–20-minute live demos, start 14-day trials, follow every
trial to a decision. Not a social-media role — content is handled by the team.

**Pay:** base (market rate, negotiable) + daily transport allowance +
**uncapped commission per paying pharmacy** + small per-trial bonus. Activity is
verified through the platform's own data, so results speak for themselves.

**Apply via WhatsApp to +255 764 591 374** with: a 1–2 page CV; a ≤2-minute voice
note answering *"A pharmacy owner says: 'I use my notebook, I don't need a
system.' What do you say?"*; your current location and earliest start date.

## Internal notes (do not publish)

- **Screener logic:** the voice-note question is the real filter. Good answers
  ask the owner questions back (expiry losses, stock-outs, being away from the
  shop) instead of listing features. Reject answers that immediately discount
  or oversell.
- **Comp guardrails:** commission is paid only on *paying* pharmacies verified in
  the admin dashboard (subscription ACTIVE), not screenshots. Trial bonus paid on
  trial accounts that record ≥1 real dispensing (filters fake signups).
- **Interview:** 30-min call + live role-play demo (share the demo environment).
  Check licence/registration status with the Pharmacy Council if they claim
  PT registration.
- **Probation:** month 1 target = 40 documented visits/week + 5 trials started;
  month 2 = first paying conversions. Decide continue/stop at day 60 on data.
